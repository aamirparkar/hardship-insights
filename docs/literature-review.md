# Literature review and product implications

Review date: 4 November 2026. Source IDs refer to the [source register](source-register.md), which records access limitations and edition details. This is a focused product review, not a systematic literature review or independent validation of publisher claims.

## Product thesis

Build a synthetic support-journey intelligence demonstrator answering: where does recorded engagement stop, what happens after a request, and which customers have observable payment stability after support? Add a small learning measurement layer to demonstrate the distinction between interaction and understanding.

The solution connects a working analytical product, reproducible engineering, statistical evaluation and an accountable operating model. Forecasting supports demand planning; predictive modelling explores outcome patterns; Power BI and Azure extend reporting and deployment. These workstreams follow the first local release and are defined in the [business roadmap](business-roadmap.md).

## Evidence to design

| Evidence theme | Project design choice | Interpretation boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Public access and internal support are distinct (S01, S04) | Separate website session and request funnels | Website activity cannot reveal all unmet need |
| Confidence and learning matter to engagement (S02, S03) | Record content version, exposure, completion, response and optional confidence | Completion is not comprehension; confidence is not demonstrated skill |
| Some reflection can be purposeful (S05) | Inspect completion time alongside knowledge responses and exits | Do not optimise for speed alone or introduce compulsory learning gates |
| Process barriers and ownership matter (S06) | Retain closure reasons, pending age and handoff events | Closed cases alone hide unresolved work |
| Assistance expiry communications matter (S07) | Record check-in/contact before planned end | An observed association does not prove contact prevented arrears |
| Capture practices change apparent trends (S07) | Version event contracts and report coverage | More recorded requests do not uniquely indicate deteriorating customer finances |

## Proposed first-release narrative

An executive sees three connected views: access and engagement, support operations, and observed outcomes. Each view shows denominators and missingness. A fictional case illustrates how a support leader investigates a cohort difference without claiming an intervention effect. Optional learning responses add context to engagement, while account outcomes remain separately measured.

Make the engineering reviewable: source-to-metric lineage, repeatable generation, tests that detect invalid data, and a run manifest. Connect findings to operational priorities, metric ownership and an evaluation proposal. Avoid proprietary index imitation and synthetic lender league tables.

## Measurement additions

Add versioned learning content and response facts, preserving anonymous participation. Use exposure and response timestamps to establish order. Optional before/after confidence requires the same question and participant linkage; report paired-response coverage and attrition. Self-reported confidence remains a separate construct from correctness.

An illustrative knowledge-response rate uses first scored responses to a versioned question divided by first scorable responses, with correct/incorrect/unknown states explicit. Report response coverage against exposed eligible sessions. Do not pool different questions or content versions into an unexplained score. Include synthetic incorrect answers and nonresponses.

Add assistance-expiry contact at event grain, then derive whether contact occurred before the planned end. Missing logs are unknown rather than proof of no contact. Do not compare contact cohorts without discussing that contact may be targeted to customers already at greater risk.

## Evidence limitations and deferred work

White papers combine conceptual arguments, commissioned surveys, case studies and product descriptions. These have different evidential strength. Original studies cited in them are not automatically verified. Synthetic estimates illustrate methods and the generator's assumptions, not actual customer benefit or business ROI.

Before an actual effectiveness claim, specify a target population, intervention, comparator, assignment method, outcome horizon, missingness strategy and customer-protection guardrails. A future randomised encouragement study could test an invitation to optional learning; operational feasibility and ethical review would be separate work.
